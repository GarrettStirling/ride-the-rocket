# Game Rules — Ride the Rocket

Quick reference for players and scorers.

## Setup
- 2+ players, two standard dice, one phone as scorekeeper.
- Choose **10** or **20** rounds before starting.
- Blank name fields become Player 1, Player 2, and so on.

## Each round
Players take turns rolling. After each roll, use the **number pad** on the app.

The **pot** is the running total for the current round — displayed large at the top. It is **blue during the opening rolls**, then ink for the rest of the round.

A **ROUND N** label flashes at the start of each round; you can still tap the pad while it shows. Last-roll text from the previous round is cleared.

### Opening rolls (first 3 rolls of every round)
| Roll | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **7** | Add **70** to the pot (lucky seven!) |
| **Anything else** | Tap the dice total — adds to the pot |
| **2× button** | Disabled — doubles don't count yet |

### After opening rolls (roll 4 and beyond)
| Roll | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **7** | Round **ends** — pot is lost, no payout. The 7 key is red. |
| **Not doubles** | Tap the dice total — adds to the pot |
| **Doubles** | Tap **2×** only — doubles the pot |
| **Snake eyes (1+1) or boxcars (6+6)** | Tap **2×** (the **2** and **12** keys are grayed out) |

## Pull out
Any player can tap **Pull out** at any time to take the **current pot** as points. They sit out the rest of the round. The pot resets to zero for everyone still in.

You collect whatever the pot was **at the moment you pulled out**. Pulled-out players sort below anyone still in.

## End of round
A round ends when:
- Someone rolls a **7** (after opening rolls), or
- **Everyone** has pulled out.

Between rounds: pull-out status resets, pot goes to 0, next round begins.

## Winning
After all rounds (10 or 20), the play chrome goes away. You see **standings**, the **winner**, and **round highlights** (highest pot, most rolls). Highest total score wins.

**New game** opens setup with the same player names and round count.

## Scorer tips
- Use **Undo** / **Redo** if you tap the wrong button.
- **2×** is grayed out during opening rolls.
- **2** and **12** are grayed out from roll 4 onward — use **2×** for snake eyes or boxcars.
- Rename, add, or remove players anytime from the player list (minimum two players).
- Leaving an in-progress game asks for confirmation; progress would be lost.
