Ride the Rocket

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026
Last updated: September 8, 2026

Ride the Rocket (“the App”) is a local dice party game scorekeeper for phones and tablets. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and what it does not collect.

Summary

The App is designed to work offline on your device. It does not require an account, and in its current form it does not collect personal information on a server or share data with advertisers or analytics providers.

Information stored on your device

The App may store the following locally on your device:

  • Player names you enter for a game
  • Current and past game scores, rounds, and related game state
  • A short history of finished games so you can replay with the same players
  • Whether you have completed the first-launch rules / onboarding screen

This data stays on your device (for example, in on-device storage such as AsyncStorage). It is not uploaded to our servers — the App currently has no backend.

Information we do not collect

At this time, the App does not:

  • Create user accounts or ask for email, phone number, or mailing address
  • Track your location
  • Access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • Use third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
  • Sell personal information

Device / system features

The App may use standard device features such as haptic feedback and your system light/dark appearance setting. Those features are processed by the operating system on your device and are not used to identify you.

Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience scorekeeper and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help you delete any relevant on-device data by guiding you to clear the App’s local storage or uninstall the App.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored game data by:

  • Clearing the App’s storage from your device settings, or
  • Uninstalling the App

California notice

If you are a California resident, note that the App does not sell personal information and, in its current form, does not collect personal information for business purposes beyond what is stored locally on your device to run the game.

Future changes (ads / purchases)

If we later add optional advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, or cloud features, we will update this Privacy Policy before those features ship and describe any new data practices clearly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or the App: support@ridetherocket.app
